# Quillo Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 20 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Quillo** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Quillo mobile application and related services (the "Service").

By using Quillo, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

---

## 1. Who we are

Quillo is a cooking and meal-planning app that helps you scan receipts, identify ingredients, and generate recipe suggestions.

**Contact:** support@quillo.app

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Account details:** email address, name (if provided), profile photo (optional).
- **Authentication:** when you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive identifiers and email as permitted by those providers.
- **Preferences:** dietary choices, cuisine preferences, household size, cooking skill level, measurement units, and notification settings.
- **Receipt scans:** photos of receipts you upload or capture with the camera.
- **Recipe data:** ingredients detected from scans, recipes generated for you, saved recipes, and shopping lists you create in the app.
- **Support messages:** information you send when contacting us.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage data:** app interactions, feature usage, scan counts, and streaks (where enabled).
- **Device information:** device type, operating system, and app version.
- **Analytics (optional):** if you consent in onboarding or settings, anonymous usage data to improve the app.
- **Advertising (free tier):** our ad partners may collect device advertising identifiers and limited usage data to show ads. See Section 8.

### 2.3 Payment information

Subscriptions are processed by **Apple App Store** or **Google Play** and managed through **RevenueCat**. We do not store your full payment card details. We receive subscription status (e.g. active, expired) to unlock premium features.

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Process receipt images with OCR and generate recipe suggestions using AI.
- Personalise recipes based on your preferences and scan history (if you enable personalisation).
- Save recipes, shopping lists, and cooking preferences on your device and account.
- Enforce daily scan limits and subscription entitlements.
- Send service-related communications (e.g. password reset).
- Improve the Service, fix bugs, and develop new features.
- Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.

---

## 4. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where applicable, we rely on:

- **Contract:** to provide the Service you signed up for.
- **Consent:** for optional analytics, personalisation, and marketing where required.
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure and improve the Service, subject to your rights.

You may withdraw consent for optional processing in **Settings** without affecting core receipt scanning, which is necessary to provide the Service.

---

## 5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Supabase** | Authentication, database, and secure file storage for receipt images |
| **Cloud OCR / AI providers** | Processing receipt images and generating recipes (via our secure backend) |
| **RevenueCat** | Subscription and entitlement management |
| **Google / Apple** | Sign-in and in-app purchases |
| **Google Mobile Ads** | Displaying ads on the free plan |
| **Service providers** | Hosting, analytics (if consented), and support tools under confidentiality obligations |

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

---

## 6. Data retention

- **Account data:** kept while your account is active. You may request deletion (see Section 9).
- **Receipt images:** stored in your account until you delete scans or your account.
- **Local data:** shopping lists and cached recipes on your device until you clear app data or uninstall.
- **Backups and logs:** retained for a limited period for security and operations, then deleted or anonymised.

---

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and row-level security on our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Children

Quillo is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in certain regions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data.

---

## 9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
- Export your data.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent for optional features.
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**In the app:** update preferences in Profile → Settings, pause or delete your account in Profile → Danger zone, or email **support@quillo.app**.

## 10. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your own, including where our providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards where required by law.
